Questions

Is it safe to use?

Every tweak is marked Safe or Moderate and shows how much impact it has. Phantom saves the original setting before it applies anything, and tweaks it can't back up are locked. Don't like a result? Restore it from the Rollback page.

How do I undo a tweak?

Open Rollback in the sidebar to restore individual tweaks, or use Revert all on the Tweaks page to undo everything you've applied.

Do I need to restart my PC?

Only some do, and those are marked “Restart required” before you apply them.

What do impact and safety mean?

Impact is how much a tweak is likely to help: high, medium or low. Safe tweaks are low-risk. Moderate ones change deeper settings, so read the description first. If you're not sure where to start, go with the high-impact Safe ones.

Is Phantom Tweaks free?

Yes, you can use it for free. Pro and Phantom unlock more tweaks.

What do Free, Pro and Phantom tweaks mean?

Every tweak is tagged with the plan it needs. Free tweaks are open to everyone, Pro adds things like the audio latency tweaks, and Phantom gets you the rest.

What does “Backup unavailable” mean?

Some tweaks change settings Phantom can't fully back up yet. They stay disabled and show “Backup unavailable”, so nothing gets changed that you can't undo.
