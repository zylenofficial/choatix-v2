Our products are digital downloads that provide immediate access upon purchase. Once accessed, they cannot be returned.

1. General Policy

Choatix V2 operates on a strict no-refund policy. All purchases of our digital products and services are final and non-refundable. This policy applies to all customers regardless of the reason for the refund request.

2. Why No Refunds?

  • Digital Nature: Our products are digital downloads that provide immediate access and cannot be physically returned.
  • Immediate Access: Once purchased, you gain instant access to our optimization tools and cannot "unuse" them.
  • Intellectual Property: Our software contains proprietary technology and trade secrets that cannot be returned.

3. What This Means

  • No Refunds: We will not process refunds for any reason, including but not limited to: dissatisfaction, change of mind, technical issues, or system incompatibility.
  • No Chargebacks: Attempting to file a chargeback or dispute with your payment provider may result in account suspension and legal action.
  • No Exchanges: We do not offer exchanges for different products or services. Each purchase is final and specific to the product selected.

4. Before You Purchase

We strongly encourage all customers to thoroughly research our products before making a purchase. This includes:

  • Reading product descriptions and features thoroughly
  • Checking system requirements and compatibility
  • Reviewing customer testimonials and reviews
  • Contacting our support team with any questions
