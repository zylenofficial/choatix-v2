Refund Policy

Digital products — all sales final

All Sales Are Final

Choatix V2 sells digital products (downloadable .exe installers). Once a purchase is completed and the download link is delivered, the product cannot be returned, exchanged, or refunded.

Why No Refunds?

  • Digital goods cannot be "returned" — the installer and tweaks are delivered instantly
  • Download links are one-time use tokens tied to your Discord username
  • All tweaks are fully documented before purchase on the Product Details page
  • The free version is available to test compatibility before buying

What If It Doesn't Work?

We don't offer refunds, but we do offer support:

  • Join our Discord and open a support ticket
  • We'll help troubleshoot: admin rights, antivirus, Windows version, driver issues
  • Every tweak has a revert command — use "Revert All" in the app if needed

Exceptions (Rare)

We may issue a refund only if:

  • Duplicate charge for the same order (contact support with both transaction IDs)
  • Payment processed but download token never generated (system error — we'll fix and resend)

By completing a purchase, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, do not purchase.
